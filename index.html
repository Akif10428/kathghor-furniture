How a custom order works

Most of our beds and wardrobes are made to measure. This is the path from message to delivery.

  1. Send your measurements

    Share the wall width and height, or visit us and we will measure your room.

  2. Approve the design

    We send a drawing with the wood, finish and price. Pay 50% to start.

  3. We build it

    Our carpenters build in the workshop. You get photos before polishing.

  4. Delivery and fitting

    We deliver, assemble and fit it in your room, then collect the balance.
